# Portal reuse, alerts and stylist records

Status: CONFIRMED user scope, 9 November 2026.

Provide private downloads on generated Consultation History looks; expose the existing-image picker in Hair, Makeup and Nail studios; hide the men's haircut only in the public/Home price list; add always-visible in-app booking alerts to Client and Stylist portals that open their appointments; and show completed services when an admin selects a stylist.

The later public-price-list clarification takes precedence over removing the catalog service globally. The new picker and appointment-link changes are authorized additions to pages previously excluded from a general redesign. Preserve protected AI resources and generation behavior. Reuse existing tables; no destructive migration, account reset, background generation or live verification booking is allowed.

Notifications must be owner/assignment scoped, show actual booking status, remain accessible while scrolling, and refresh when the visible page returns to focus. Read markers may remain browser-local and must be separated by account. Admin stylist history must show completed services only and keep null prices distinct from zero. Image reuse passes existing private bytes to the established upload flow.
