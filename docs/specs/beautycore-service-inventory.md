# Salon inventory and completed-service stock reporting

Status: CONFIRMED user scope, 9 November 2026. Preserve existing records and all protected AI/model, data, generation, inference, deployment and release resources.

Extend the existing inventory and movement ledger with optional product metadata, suppliers, service-ID requirements, exact fractional quantities, immutable movement details and completed-service consumption. Do not seed the reference catalog, infer purchase costs from historical selling prices, consume historical services or reset inventory. Suggested products are configurable templates only.

The user's later clarification takes precedence: staff need to record only products that were used up after a service, without listing every product used. Default to reported-only completion. Staff choose an inventory product and quantity; its stored unit and identity are retained. Only explicitly reported consumable quantities are deducted when completion commits. Omitted supplies retain stock, even when they have service planning requirements. An empty report is valid. Duplicate completion must not deduct again. Equipment is excluded from this form and rejected as a reported consumable by the backend. This form is part of confirming service completion; later corrections use the administrator's linked stock correction workflow.

Retain optional administrator-selected configured-consumption mode for the original full-requirements workflow. That mode prefills service estimates and requires explanations for changed/additional quantities. Requirements are independent of service prices/durations and also support shortage planning. Do not change the 90-minute booking rule.

All stock writes must append an audit movement with actor, quantity, before/after balance and reason where required. Receipts add supplier, cost, reference and received date; corrections link completed appointments and never rewrite original history. Use request UUIDs for retry safety, row/advisory locks for concurrency, and atomic completion plus deductions. Default shortages block completion; optional consume-available policy records unmet quantities without negative stock. Expired/inactive items are unavailable for normal consumption. Tools/equipment are reusable and never automatically deducted.

Preserve legacy count units; lock unit/type after movements or service configuration. Allow exact conversions only between grams/kilograms and milliliters/liters, with at most three fractional quantity digits. Purchase costs refer to the product's stored stock unit. Unknown costs remain unknown; inventory valuation and expense summaries identify unpriced rows. Existing legacy usage notes retain their meaning and do not imply stock consumption.

Administrators manage products, images, suppliers, requirements, policy, receipts, withdrawals, physical-count adjustments, attention views, immutable history and actual-record reports. Staff can inspect supplies and requirements, and report used-up products through the authorized completion form. Clients cannot manage inventory or view private purchase costs. Products with history are archived instead of deleted. Filters cover product, category, stock/type/status, transaction kind, supplier, staff and salon-date ranges. Desktop/mobile layouts must use the existing portal design.

Verify roles, stock bounds, units, expiry, idempotence, races, rollback, report totals, retention, API transport and actual UI interactions using an isolated database. A configured-database upgrade must fingerprint all pre-existing guarded tables before and after its transaction. Visual browser acceptance is separate from DOM tests and must not be claimed when blocked.
